# Third-party / original asset attribution

## Fonts

Self-hosted, subsetted WOFF2 files:

- Space Grotesk — Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License. License: `public/fonts/spacegrotesk-OFL.txt`.
- Inter — Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License. License: `public/fonts/inter-OFL.txt`.
- IBM Plex Mono — Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License. License: `public/fonts/ibmplexmono-OFL.txt`.

Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk , https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter , https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono . Local subsets retain Latin text, punctuation, and the symbols needed by this interface. No font CDN is used at runtime.

## Globe data

Land-point samples were generated from the public world GeoJSON in the D3 Graph Gallery data collection:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/holtzy/D3-graph-gallery/master/DATA/world.geojson

The site renders a geometric, illustrative globe. It makes no claim of live attack geography, actual threat origins, tracking, precise territorial boundaries, or geolocation of visitors. The fallback SVG is generated from the same point samples.

- Vite / esbuild / TypeScript / Playwright / axe-core — their respective upstream development-tool licenses.

No official EC-Council, RedTeam, CompTIA credential seal, certificate scan, or invented credential verification badge is used. The credential symbols and project artwork are original interface SVG/CSS graphics, not reproductions of official certificates.
